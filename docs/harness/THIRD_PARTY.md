# Third-party harness components

The installer downloads Superpowers and gstack at the revisions pinned in
`config/harness/agents.json`. Their source, licenses and notices remain in the
ignored `.harness/tools/` checkouts. Project skill links are also local and ignored.

The `.specify/` scaffold was generated by GitHub Spec Kit `specify-cli 1.0.7`
(https://github.com/github/spec-kit), under the MIT license. The project-specific
constitution was written for this repository. Third-party frameworks are installed
as dependencies into independent environments and retain their own licenses.

No framework being configured here implies endorsement or statistical validation
of this project's stock models. See the per-tool catalog and verification report.
